<section id="research" class="section research-section" aria-labelledby="research-title">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="section-index">01 / Research</p>
    <h2 id="research-title">Questions worth <em>asking.</em></h2>
  </div>
  <div class="research-intro">
    <p class="lead">The microbes that live with us are in constant conversation with our immune system. I want to understand that conversation well enough to make it useful.</p>
    <p>My interests bring together computational models, experiments, and molecular design. I’m especially interested in the host–microbe interface: where a microbial gene or protein becomes an immune signal, and where a mechanistic insight can become a new way to immunize.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="question-grid">
    <article class="question-card">
      <span class="question-number">I</span>
      <h3>Discover</h3>
      <p>Which microbial factors change immune behavior? I’m interested in model-guided discovery of previously overlooked immunomodulatory biology at the host–pathogen and host–commensal interface.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="question-card">
      <span class="question-number">II</span>
      <h3>Design</h3>
      <p>How might we engineer proteins that help beneficial microbes act as vaccines? I’m interested in designs that strengthen and direct immunization through commensal bacteria.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="question-card">
      <span class="question-number">III</span>
      <h3>Measure</h3>
      <p>Why does the immune system respond strongly to some targets and tolerate others? I want to develop screening technologies that make tolerance and immunodominance easier to study.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>

<section id="story" class="section story-section" aria-labelledby="story-title">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="section-index">03 / The path here</p>
    <h2 id="story-title">A little <em>context.</em></h2>
  </div>
  <div class="story-layout">
    <p class="story-lead">I came to biology through computation, and stayed for the questions that only experiments can answer.</p>
    <div class="story-copy">
      <p>I first explored molecular machine learning through open-source work on <a href="https://deepchem.io/">DeepChem</a> and research with <a href="https://www.matter.toronto.edu/">Alan Aspuru-Guzik</a> in Toronto. At <a href="https://www.berkeley.edu/">UC Berkeley</a>, I studied computer science and bioengineering and worked in <a href="https://doudnalab.org/">Jennifer Doudna’s lab</a> on RNA and protein design. Along the way, research at Microsoft and the <a href="https://evodesign.org/">Laboratory of Evolutionary Design</a> taught me how much I enjoy moving between models and biological mechanisms.</p>
      <p>Outside the lab, I helped lead research at <a href="https://ml.berkeley.edu/">Machine Learning at Berkeley</a> and co-organized a BioML seminar series. I care about making research more legible and welcoming to people finding their way into the field.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
